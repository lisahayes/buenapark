East Buena Park Questions

Three houses on our street have repiped this year. Should we?

Treat it as a forecast worth verifying, not a verdict. Same-plan, same-era houses age together, and a street's repipe wave is real data, but your specific system's condition decides: repair history, pressure, what visible pipe shows, what any removed sections have shown. The assessment turns the street's pattern into your house's answer, without the scare pitch.

Why does our hallway floor get warm every winter?

Seasonal warmth that tracks heating-season hot water use, the long slab run to the back bathroom working harder, can be innocent conduction. Warmth paired with a moving meter, a rising gas bill, or growth over weeks is the slab leak's classic introduction. The five-minute meter test separates the two, and winter is exactly when the east side's marginal joints choose to open.
