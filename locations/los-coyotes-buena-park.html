Roots, Respected and Managed

Los Coyotes Questions

Can our lateral be fixed without destroying the parkway tree?

Usually, and the footage decides how. Measured camera work places the defect precisely, and the repair options run from root maintenance through spot excavation beside the root zone to trenchless replacement between pits. The tree and the lateral have coexisted for decades; the goal of good sewer work here is another few decades of the same.

Does a bigger house really change the repipe math?

It does, in both directions. More bathrooms and longer runs mean more copper aging and more repair events accumulating, which pulls the crossover earlier. The same size makes the one-time repipe price larger, which owners feel. The honest answer is the house-specific arithmetic: repair history and pipe condition against both quotes, on paper.
