Manufactured Homes: A Different Plumbing Animal

A manufactured home's plumbing lives in the open air beneath the unit: supply lines crossing from the park's riser to the home's belly, flexible connectors doing work that rigid pipe does elsewhere, and drain runs suspended to the park's sewer connection. The failure profile follows the construction, connector and fitting failures lead, belly-wrap moisture hides slow leaks against the underside insulation, and the park-to-home boundary adds its own attribution line: the riser and its shutoff typically belong to the park, everything homeward of it to the owner. Diagnosis is refreshingly direct, the underside is inspectable the way a crawl space is, and repairs run economical for the same access reasons, but the materials and connections are their own trade knowledge, and crews unfamiliar with them improvise expensively.
