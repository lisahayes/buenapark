South Buena Park Questions

Pool level drops a quarter inch a day. Normal for the south side?

In warm, dry, or windy weather, right at the edge of normal evaporation, which is why the bucket comparison exists: pool against bucket over 24 hours, pump running normally. Losing meaningfully more than the bucket convicts a leak; matching it acquits. Sudden changes matter most, a pool that held all spring and started dropping did not change climates.

Our wet spot is between the pool and the house. Which system is it?

The three-way question, and isolation answers it without digging: house valve closed against the meter, pool equipment isolated and pressure-tested, irrigation zones checked in turn. Water surfaces downhill of its escape, so the spot's location suggests less than people hope; the tests name the system in an hour.

Pool country's classic puzzle. Isolation names the system before anything gets dug.
