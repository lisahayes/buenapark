Mall Area Questions

Does freeway vibration really cause leaks?

It does not cause them so much as it refuses to let marginal joints rest: constant low-level vibration accelerates the fatigue that age and hard water begin. The effect is a weighting factor, not a verdict, near-freeway homes with compounding leak histories reach the reroute conversation a little sooner, and the same house two blocks into the grid gets a little longer.

We lease a pad space and found water. Whose problem is it?

The lease decides the money; the isolation decides the facts. With adequate valving we attach the finding to a specific space or common line and document it identically for tenant and landlord, which converts the who-pays conversation from argument to paperwork. Getting the facts nailed first is in everyone's interest, including the relationship's.
