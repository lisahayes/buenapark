What to Do First If You Smell Gas

Leave the house immediately. Do not flip a light switch, use a phone indoors, or search for the source with a lighter. If you can reach the meter without lingering, shut the gas off with a wrench, a quarter turn so the valve sits crosswise to the pipe. Call SoCalGas from outside, then call (714) 750-8637. Only SoCalGas can restore service to the meter once a repair is complete, which is why we coordinate with them directly rather than around them.

Why Buena Park Gas Lines Fail

The steel gas lines running to Buena Park's boom era tract homes are often the same age as the copper supply lines now in the pinhole failure window from decades of Orange County Groundwater Basin's hard water. Decades in the ground and appliance connectors that outlived their rated service life go unnoticed until something gives. Buena Park's mix of sandy alluvial soil and expansive clay pockets near the Whittier and Norwalk fault zones shifts with the seasons the same way it stresses water lines, working joints and fittings loose over time.

How We Locate the Leak

Once SoCalGas confirms it is safe to proceed, we pressure test the line in sections and use a combustible gas detector along every joint and connection. Buried yard lines get traced with the same acoustic methods we use to find buried water leaks, marking the failure point instead of trenching the whole run blind. For sub-slab gas lines, we supplement with electronic leak detection to confirm the pipe route before further work.

Common Failure Points

Flexible appliance connectors to a stove, dryer, or water heater have a service life, and a common failure point is a fixture swapped with the old connector reused. Meter sets on 1950s tract homes sometimes carry thread sealant never rated for current line pressure. Repairs beyond a simple connector swap are permitted work, and we pull those permits so the fix is documented and inspected. Call (714) 750-8637.

Gas Leak Detection & Repair Questions

Is it safe to stay inside if I smell a little gas?

No. Leave immediately and call from outside. A faint smell can concentrate quickly in an enclosed space, and there is no reliable way to judge severity by nose alone.

Will SoCalGas find the leak for me?

SoCalGas responds to reported leaks and can shut off service if needed, but locating the exact failure point and completing the repair is licensed plumbing work, which is where we come in.

How much does gas leak repair cost in Buena Park?

It depends entirely on where the leak is. An appliance connector swap is usually a same visit fix. A buried service line replacement is a bigger job. We quote in writing after locating the leak.

What Affects the Cost of This Service?

Every job on this service starts with a free on-site assessment, and the price gets confirmed before any work begins. Three factors move the number: the system's location (under a slab, inside a wall, or buried in the yard), its material and access quality, and how many independent techniques are needed to reach a confident locate. A straightforward slab locate on accessible copper in a tract home is a different scope from the same diagnosis in a two-story with restricted access. We give you the specific price for your specific job, not an average from a brochure.

California slab leak detection typically runs $200 to $500; spot repairs typically range from $2,000 to $3,500 depending on access and finish restoration. Reroutes and repiping carry higher up-front costs and lower long-run costs. Where insurance covers the damage portion of a leak event, our written finding with photos is the paperwork adjusters ask for, and we produce it as a standard deliverable.
