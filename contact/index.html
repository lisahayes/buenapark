Before We Arrive

If water is actively flowing, shut off the main valve at the meter box, usually at the sidewalk or property line. For a suspected water heater leak, close the cold-side valve on top of the tank. If you can do it safely, note where you saw or heard the water first. That single detail often saves our technician twenty minutes of isolation testing.
