Buena Park Leak Cost Calculator Questions

How much does a dripping faucet cost per year in Buena Park?

A faucet dripping at 60 drips per minute wastes about 5.4 gallons per day. At City of Buena Park water rates including the 10% Capital Charge, that is roughly $9 to $11 per year. A fast drip at 120 per minute roughly doubles that. Small alone, but add a running toilet and the cost climbs fast.

What is the Capital Charge on a Buena Park water bill?

The Capital Charge is a surcharge equal to 10% of the sum of your Capacity Charge and Commodity Charge, added to every monthly bill. It covers infrastructure maintenance and capital improvement costs. Because it applies to the entire variable portion of your bill, the true cost of every additional gallon you use is 10% higher than the published commodity rate alone.

What are City of Buena Park water rates?

City of Buena Park water bills have three components: a fixed Capacity Charge by meter size, a tiered Commodity Charge per HCF (hundred cubic feet) of use, and a 10% Capital Charge on top of both. Download the current rate resolution at buenapark.com or call the Water Division at (714) 562-3721 for the current per-HCF rates.

Does Buena Park have tiered water pricing?

Yes. The City of Buena Park uses a two-tier commodity rate structure. Water used up to the Tier 1 threshold each month is priced at the lower Tier 1 rate. Usage above that threshold is priced at the higher Tier 2 rate. Both tiers also have the 10% Capital Charge applied on top.
