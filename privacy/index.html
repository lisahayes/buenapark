Overview

This privacy policy explains how Buena Park Leak Repair Experts ("we", "us") handles information when you visit buenaparkleakrepairpros.com or call our business line. We keep this simple because our data practices are simple: this is a static informational website with no contact forms, no user accounts, and no comment systems.

Information We Collect

Phone calls. When you call (714) 750-8637, standard call metadata (your phone number, call time, and duration) is processed by our call routing provider to connect you with a service technician. Calls may be recorded for quality and dispatch accuracy; where required, you will hear a notice at the start of the call.

Website analytics. Our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring URLs. We use this in aggregate to understand which pages are useful. This site does not set advertising cookies and does not run third-party ad trackers.

How Information Is Used

Call information is used to dispatch service, follow up on your request, and maintain records of work performed. Aggregate site data is used to improve content. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers needed to route calls and host this website.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may request details about personal information we hold, request deletion, and opt out of any sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, call (714) 750-8637 and state that you are making a privacy request; we will verify your identity using the phone number associated with your records.

Data Retention

Call records and job records are retained as long as needed for warranty, accounting, and legal purposes, then deleted or anonymized. Server logs rotate on our hosting provider's standard schedule.

Third-Party Links

Pages on this site may reference public resources such as the City of Buena Park or the Orange County Water District. Their sites carry their own privacy policies.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and revise the date below. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
